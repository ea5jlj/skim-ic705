# Common Spanish words for the word-spacing fix (src/words.c), as people send
# them in CW conversations. Written for this project; tools/mkdict.py expands
# it and joins it with the English list. Accents are removed and Ñ becomes N
# (AÑO → ANO), as in CW, where the decoder only gives A-Z.
#
# Lines:
#   = w w w        words as they are
#   n: w w w       nouns: plural added (casa → casas, ciudad → ciudades)
#   a: w w w       adjectives: feminine and plurals (bueno → buena buenos buenas)
#   v: w w w       regular verbs: the usual forms (present, past, imperfect,
#                  future, conditional, present subjunctive, imperative,
#                  gerund, participle); "pensar/ie", "poder/ue", "pedir/i"
#                  mark a stem change in the stressed forms
#   Irregular verbs are written out with "=".
