# Common English words for the word-spacing fix (src/words.c), as people send
# them in CW conversations. Written for this project; tools/mkdict.py expands
# it and joins it with the Spanish list.
#
# Lines:
#   = w w w        words as they are
#   n: w w w       nouns: plural added (rig → rigs, box → boxes, city → cities)
#   v: w w w       regular verbs: -s, -ed, -ing (work works worked working);
#                  "stop/2" doubles the last letter (stopped stopping)
#   a: w w w       adjectives as they are; "cold/er" adds -er -est
#                  (colder coldest), "big/2er" doubles (bigger biggest)
#   Irregular forms are written out with "=".
